# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be respectful. We are here to make hardware work, not to score points.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a missing detail, not bad intent.
- Be patient with people who are new to HID, Rust, or open source. Everyone
  reading a report descriptor for the first time is lost.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Respect that maintainers do this in their spare time, and that a device you
  own may be one nobody else can test.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing someone's
private information, or persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue marked private-if-possible, or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this
document, and may ban repeat offenders.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
